# Game ideas

Looking for something to build? Pick one, open a **Game idea** issue (or
comment on an existing one) so nobody duplicates work, then follow
[games/README.md](../games/README.md). Ideas are grouped by Yantrika pillar.
Feel free to invent your own.

## Learn
* **Resistor colour codes**: read the bands, pick the value.
* **PWM explorer**: change the duty cycle and watch a motor or LED respond.
* **Logic gates**: wire AND/OR/NOT to light a lamp.
* **Serial protocols**: guess which of I2C, SPI or UART fits a sensor.

## Build
* **Line follower tuner**: adjust P, I and D and see a robot follow a track.
* **Pinout puzzle**: connect parts to the right Arduino Nano pins.
* **Power budget**: choose a battery for a bot without browning out.
* **Debug the sketch**: find the bug in a short Arduino program.

## Compete
* **Sorting race**: predict which algorithm finishes first.
* **Path planner duel**: BFS, A* or greedy: who reaches the goal fastest?
* **Stag hunt / chicken**: more game theory against bots.
* **Speed wiring**: connect a circuit against the clock.

## Share
* **Explain it back**: pick the clearest explanation of a concept for a younger student.
* **Workshop planner**: schedule volunteers, kits and rooms for a school visit.
* **Myth buster**: true or false questions about robots and AI.

## Improve what exists
* Translate a game's text into Bengali or Hindi.
* Add keyboard controls or a high-contrast mode to a game.
* Add a harder level to Maze Finder or Auction Arena.
