# Code of Conduct

Yantrika is a student-led club and this project is a place to learn. Everyone
taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to:

* **Be respectful and welcoming**, whatever someone's background, gender,
  language, age, experience or ability.
* **Give kind, specific feedback** on work, and receive it graciously.
  Questions from beginners are always welcome.
* **Assume good intent**, and ask when something is unclear.
* **Keep it safe for school students**: no harassment, discrimination, sexual
  content, doxxing, or personal attacks.

Contributions that break these expectations may be removed, and repeated or
serious problems can lead to a ban from the project.

**Reporting:** email contact@yantrika.in with links or screenshots. Reports are
read by the maintainers and handled privately.
